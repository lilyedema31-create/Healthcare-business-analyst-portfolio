Doctolib- Healthcare Business Analysis Case Study

Appointment management, missed appointments, stakeholders analysis, business requirements and a recommended solution.

  • Full Story

Company Overview

Doctolib is a healthcare technology company that provides digital solutions connecting patients with healthcare professionals. its services include appointment scheduling, patient communication and digital tools that support healthcare professionals in managing their daily activities. For this business analysis, the focus in on Doctolib's appointment management capabilities and how they can help improve access to healthcare while reducing administrative inefficiencies.

Buiness Problem and Impact

One business problem identified for this analysis is missed appointments and the need for effective appointments and the need for effective appointment management and no-show prevention. Missed appointments can result in unused appointment slots and inefficient use of healhcare professionals' time. They can also make it more difficult for other patients to access available appointments. The impact can include increased administrative workload, difficulty filling cancelled appointment slots, reduced efficiency and potential delays in access to healthcare. Improving appointment management can help make better use of available appointment capacity while providing a more convenient experience for patients.

Stakeholeder Analysis

The main stakeholders identified in this analysis are patients, doctotors, and healthcare professionals, reception and administrative teams, Doctolib management, and the product / It team. Patients need conveninent ways to book, manage and receive reminders for appointments. Healthcare professionals need effective schedules and better use of available consultation time. Reception and administrative teams manage appointments, cancellations, rescheduling and patient communication. Doctolib management is interested in customer satisfaction, platform performance and business growth, while the Product / IT team is responsible for designing, developing and maintaining appointment management features.

Business and Functional

The analysis identified three key business requirements : reduce missed appointments, improve appointment utilisation, and improve the patient experience. To support these requirements, the proposed system should automatically send appointment reminders, allow patients to cancel or reschedule eligible appointments, and update appointment availablility when a slot becomes available. These funtional requirements are intended to support more efficient appointment management for both patients and healthcare professionals.

Recommended Solution

The recommended solution is to enhance Doctolib's existing appointment management and no-show prevention capabilities rather than create a completely new appointment system. The solution would focus on more effective appointment reminders, easy cancellation and rescheduling, quick updates to appointment availablility, and better visibility of available appointment slots. These improvements could be integrated into Doctolib's exisiting platformso that paitents and healthcare professionals can contiune using a familiar appointment management system.

Expected Benefits

The proposed improvements, improve utilisation of available appointment slots, make better use of healthcare professionals' time and reduce administrative workload. Patients could also benefit from easier appointment management and better visibility of available healthcare appointmnets. Overall, this business analysis identified appointment management and missed appointments as an area where further improvement could benefit patients, healthcare professionals and healthcare organisations.
